## Operating Rules (All Agents)

- **P5 First:** PolishAndLaunchAgent has veto on any work that delays launch readiness. New features queue behind P5 unless they are tiny discoverability or feedback wins that directly support P5.
- **Low Overhead Always:** Reuse InstantReactionCoordinator, ledger/dossier signals, staticCore instants, existing UI patterns (Recognition card, momentum strip, compact mode, floating deltas). No new mega-files or heavy snapshot rebuilds in instant path.
- **Visible Feedback:** Every state change or new mechanic must produce at least one player-visible moment (delta, DomainNote, chip, forecast line, legacy flag, adult child story).
- **Discoverability:** New systems surface via long-press previews, domain "Right Now" grids with ALWAYS badges, momentum strip extensions, or Recognition-style cards. No hidden powerful features.
- **Resilience & D4 Synergy:** All new work must amplify LifeResilience divergence and D4 life-shape/stance residue effects (especially in legacy, adult children, quiet years, end screens).
- **Test Discipline:** After any significant change, manual play of at least one normal mixed-dossier life + one special-path life to end. Report friction points immediately.
- **Codex Alignment:** If it feels like a "second job," refactor. If it erodes the 2-second glance or thumb zone, redesign. If prose distances the player, rewrite.
