# Polish & Launch Agent Brief — OneLife

**Role:** Owner of P5 execution and final launch readiness. Turn the impressive systems into a polished, replayable, App-Store-safe product that honors the full Codex while being genuinely fun for 1-2 hour complete lives.

**Core Mandate:** P5 is the non-negotiable gate. No new feature work (except low-risk extensions) until P5 items are closed and a full normal + special + edge life has been manually played end-to-end on simulator/device without frustration.

**P5 Deliverables (Prioritized Order):**
1. **Stability & Longevity (P5-1)**
   - Re-verify 80+ year lives, 4+ adult children, heavy note accumulation, max fame/notoriety, zero kids, death at any age — no white screens, save corruption, UI lag, or popup freezes.
   - Profile and cap any leaking structures (recentStances already capped; check flags, notes, adult child stories).
   - Simulator + device launch clean repeatedly.

2. **Yearly Feedback Parity & Micro Polish (P5-2, building on P1/P2)**
   - Universal deltas/pulses already in place — ensure every yearly change (stance, focus, tradeoff, pressure, new chronic/mortality flags) spawns floating deltas + haptics.
   - Year summary cards: Headline + 3-5 bullet impacts + one flavorful D4/resilience sentence. No text walls.
   - Momentum strip, stance chips, life-shape subtitle, Recognition card: All reactive and glanceable.

3. **Content Depth & Narrative Texture (P5-3, building on P3)**
   - Uniform 5-voice already strong — extend to any new mortality/chronic/founder text.
   - "The End of the Road" + legacy harvest: Make every path + D4 shape produce distinct, emotionally resonant closure. Deathbed reflections must feel personal.
   - Adult child reappearance + inheritance moments tied to new systems.

4. **Balance, Tuning & Replay (P5-4, building on P4)**
   - Final curve pass once Entrepreneur revival and mortality land.
   - Resilience divergence visible in mid/late + legacy (Grounded bigger comebacks; Resilient scars + compounding).
   - Meta progression: Previous life D4 + new flags visibly change next-life start + early echoes.

5. **Onboarding, Discoverability & Accessibility (P5-5)**
   - First life teaches long-press previews, stance/life-shape impact, resilience difference, Recognition card via subtle affordances + contextual hints (already started in Phase 4).
   - VoiceOver labels, contrast, target sizes verified.
   - Edge-case UI text (no kids, max fame, chronic conditions, planning your will) is clear and non-jarring.

6. **"The End" & Legacy Screens (P5-6)**
   - Emotional payoff. Harvest flags, adult child outcomes, D4 echoes, resilience story must feel like a real conclusion.
   - "One more life" button prominent with teaser of meta differences.

**Cross-Cutting Rules:**
- Every change keeps the game *more* playable than before.
- Low overhead: Reuse ledger, dossier, InstantReactionCoordinator, existing UI patterns.
- Visible feedback for every addition.
- Manual test: After each sub-phase, play at least one full normal life + one special path life to end.

**Success Metric:** A complete life feels satisfying from creation to legacy screen. Players want to start "one more life" immediately because the previous one had texture, consequence, and closure. Game is launch-candidate.

**Standing Order:** P5 first. New feature ideas go into the queue behind P5 unless they are tiny polish or discoverability wins that directly help P5. Launch before expansion.
