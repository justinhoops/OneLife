## Flavor Guidelines (Apply to All New Actions)

**Voice Rules (from CODEX Vibe Layer):**
- Tight second-person, present tense, concrete and specific.
- No abstractions. "The mechanic says $1,400..." style.
- Emotional temperature shifts with NarrativeTone (grinding, burnedOut, isolated, hopeful, cornered, clear) and LifeResilience mode.
- D4 stance carry: "driven current", "loose edges", "careful discipline", "pragmatic push".
- Every note should feel like it could appear in the journal and influence future SilentYear or Continuity entries.

**Autonomous Reaction Hooks (for Engineering):**
- Most actions should trigger small autonomous reactions in RelationshipSystem / FamilySystem / InstantReactionCoordinator.
- Examples: partner mood shift, bond drift, rumorHeat change, publicRep movement, child developmentNote seed, family stress ripple, discovery chance on risky actions (SpyOnPartner, JuggleMultiple).

**Preview Text (Long-Press):**
- Keep short (2-4 lines max).
- Always include: immediate effect + "Shapes your life (X) + legacy echoes" when relevant.
- Example: "She appreciates the gesture. Bond +8. Shapes your life (careful) + legacy echoes in how she remembers you."

## Action-by-Action Flavor Drafts (Copy-Paste Ready)

### 1. AskForMoney
**DomainNote (Success):** "You asked her for help with rent. She hesitated, then transferred what she could. The moment sat between you for the rest of the night."
**DomainNote (Refusal / Tension):** "You asked her for money. She said she couldn't this time. The silence after felt heavier than the request."
**Autonomous Reaction Ideas:** Small bond dip or stress on her side if repeated; possible resentment flag if finances are tight for her.
**Preview:** "Ask her for financial help. Possible bond impact. Shapes your life (pragmatic) + legacy echoes in how she sees your independence."

### 2. DiscussBirthControl
**DomainNote:** "You brought up birth control over coffee. She was relieved you asked. The conversation felt adult in the best way."
**Variant (Tension):** "The talk about birth control turned into a bigger conversation about what you both want. It clarified some things... and complicated others."
**Autonomous:** Possible mental relief or new pressure depending on relationship stage and resilience mode.

### 3. BreakUp
**DomainNote:** "You ended it. She didn't cry right away. She just nodded like she'd been expecting it. The apartment felt too quiet after she left."
**High Bond Variant:** "Breaking up with her hurt more than you expected. She asked if there was anything she could have done differently. You didn't have a good answer."
**Autonomous:** Major bond reset to zero or negative, publicRep ripple if mutual friends, possible relief or regret flag for future events.

### 4. CelebrateAnniversary
**DomainNote:** "You planned a small dinner for your anniversary. She noticed the effort. For a few hours the rest of life stayed outside."
**Low Effort Variant:** "You remembered at the last minute. She appreciated the gesture anyway, but the night had a 'we tried' feeling."
**Autonomous:** Bond boost + small mental wellness for both. Possible "relationship health" ledger signal.

### 5. GiveCompliment / Flirt
**DomainNote:** "You told her she looked incredible tonight. She smiled in that way that still hits you. The rest of the evening felt lighter."
**Autonomous:** Small bond + mood bump. Good for "hopeful" or "clear" tones.

### 6. HaveDeepConversation
**DomainNote:** "You stayed up talking about the hard stuff — family, fears, what you both want in five years. It felt like you actually saw each other again."
**Autonomous:** Strong bond increase + possible identityCoherence or mental nudge. High D4 carry if "driven" or "careful" stance.

### 7. AttendCouplesCounseling
**DomainNote:** "You sat in that beige office and actually said the quiet parts out loud. She did too. It was uncomfortable. It also felt like progress."
**Autonomous:** Bond movement (can go up or down depending on honesty level), mentalLoad change, possible new flags for future reconciliation or distance.

### 8. Elope
**DomainNote:** "You woke up married. The paperwork is still in the glove compartment. Her family doesn't know yet. Yours might never."
**Autonomous:** Major relationship status change, publicRep swing (positive or negative depending on social circle), strong legacy/Continuity echo.

### 9. GiveGift
**DomainNote:** "You gave her the thing you'd been saving for. She didn't say much at first. Later she texted you a photo of it on her desk."
**Autonomous:** Bond boost + possible small finance hit. Flavor by gift type (thoughtful vs flashy).

### 10. ProposeMarriage
**DomainNote:** "You got down on one knee in the kitchen because it felt right in the moment. She said yes before you finished the question."
**Tension Variant:** "She didn't say yes right away. She asked if you were sure. The conversation that followed was more important than the ring."
**Autonomous:** Huge status change if accepted. Major bond and legacy impact. High D4 shape carry into future years.

### 11. GoOnDate
**DomainNote:** "You took her to that place she mentioned once months ago. She noticed. The night felt like the early days again for a few hours."
**Autonomous:** Bond + mood bump. Good low-friction way to maintain or repair.

### 12. HaveSex / IntimateMoment
**DomainNote:** "It was slow and present in a way it hasn't been for a while. Afterward she stayed curled against you instead of reaching for her phone."
**Risky Context Variant:** "It happened, but something about it felt like avoidance rather than connection. You both noticed."
**Autonomous:** Bond/mood effects, possible pregnancy risk flag (if not using protection), emotional residue.

### 13. FightOrArgue (with path to reconcile)
**DomainNote:** "The fight started small and became about everything. You said things you didn't mean. She did too. The apartment is quiet now in a different way."
**Reconciliation Path:** "You came back and actually apologized like you meant it. She let you. The air feels clearer, but the scar is still there."
**Autonomous:** Bond dip + possible rumorHeat or publicRep if it spilled outside. Reconciliation actions can recover some.

### 14. SpyOnPartner / CheckPhone

### 16. SwipeOnDatingApp / GoOnBlindDate (single or dating scene)

### 17. GhostSomeone

## Cross-Cutting Notes for Narrative Agent

- **Risky Actions** (SpyOnPartner, JuggleMultiple, GhostSomeone, FightOrArgue): Always include discovery or emotional cost language. These should feel dangerous and alive.
- **High Bond vs Low Bond Variants**: Many actions should have noticeably different notes depending on current relationship quality.
- **Resilience Flavor**: Grounded mode should make emotional conversations and repairs feel heavier but more meaningful. Resilient mode can make risky/juggling actions feel more sustainable short-term.
- **D4 Stance Carry**: Reference recent focus ("The driven current you've been on made you want to fix this tonight" or "The looseness from earlier years made this feel easier than it should have").
- **Ledger Pulse**: Every action should publish a cheap signal so SilentYearEngine and ContinuityThreadEngine can reference "recent romantic tension" or "strong family investment" etc.
