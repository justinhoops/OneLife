# Living Cast implementation phases

## Phase 1 — Passive family appraisal

Family contacts hold a personality, bond, stance, and last judgement. They
passively react to care, sustained absence, and a new felony case. Dependent
children also register family focus or work-only years. These updates are
lightweight, deterministic, and surface only on a meaningful stance change.

## Phase 2 — Dynamic cast activation (COMPLETE)

Contextual NPCs now have stable identities distinct from their current role,
activity states, and bounded remembered interactions. Existing role-based
actions resolve through the highest-relevance cast member for compatibility.
Dormant people hibernate and reactivate when an action calls for their role.
The next phase adds caused events that can select those dormant members.

## Phase 3 — Cause-and-effect situations (COMPLETE)

Caused situations now select an eligible cast member only when their history
and current pressure make an encounter plausible. Hostile people can exploit
career, reputation, or legal exposure; dormant supportive people can return
during genuine financial stress; independently successful contacts can open a
career door; and a promotion can expose jealousy in a high-drive relationship.
Each member has a four-year cooldown, one event can surface per year, and
choices write back to that person's rapport and memories. Support stays modest
rather than becoming a rescue mechanism.

## Phase 4 — Hidden personality and independent lives (COMPLETE)

Cast members separate durable Bond from present-day relationship tone.
Personality observations unlock through interaction instead of displaying the
underlying profile. Temporary circumstances alter reactions without rewriting
permanent identity. Relevant people advance a compressed independent life;
dormant people hibernate and catch up deterministically only when context
brings them back.

## Phase 5 — Current-life presentation and biography (COMPLETE)

The dashboard distills the three most important current domain signals instead
of presenting a yearly feed. Biography-grade milestones persist separately from
the bounded full journal, so a long life keeps its defining beginning, turning
points, and ending.

## Phase 6 — Cast breadth and domain hooks (COMPLETE)

The cast now has authored situations for family health and legal boundaries,
ordinary-work pressure, special-career sponsorship, diamond-career scrutiny,
crime accountability, and health check-ins. A friend can also vouch directly
to a boss, writing the decision back to both people rather than treating a
network as a generic bonus. Every situation still requires a visible cause,
uses individual cooldowns, and resolves through the owning Family, Social, or
existing effect domain.

## Phase 7 — Current-life cast UI (COMPLETE)

The Love tab now includes a People glance without adding a fifth domain or a
second relationship action loop. It reports the active-circle count and the
tone of the two most relevant connections. Its read-only sheet keeps the three
people shaping the current chapter above the wider dormant circle, then uses
progressive disclosure for current circumstances, independent-life chapters,
learned traits, connection history, and remembered moments. Private goals do
not appear as meters or tasks. The view
consumes the directory's existing relevance order and never recalculates or
mutates Social state.

## Phase 8 — Character combinations and quiet goals (COMPLETE)

Every contextual NPC now receives two to four compatible stable tags, one or
two private goals, and a typed connection history. Repeated interaction builds
bounded evidence; only supported personality observations and stable tags are
shown to the player. Goals advance inside compressed independent-life beats,
hibernate with dormant people, and surface only through completed-life changes
rather than a progress meter.

Career, Education, Finance, Health, Family, and Asset outcomes emit typed life
signals. Social interprets those facts through each person's personality,
stable tags, current circumstances, relationship history, and independent
standing. The same crisis can therefore draw loyalty from one person and
distance from another. Content predicates can target combinations of tags,
circumstances, goals, connections, memories, life chapter, bond, rapport, and
time since contact. Save v41 deterministically backfills older cast members
without consuming gameplay randomness.

## Next phase — Authored combination breadth

Playtest the existing hooks and the new People surface before adding authored
variants. Expand only pressures that recur or feel causally thin in reviewed
lives; do not turn the cast into a maintenance chore or create generic social
events without a remembered cause.
