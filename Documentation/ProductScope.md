# Release scope

## Included

- Four primary domains: School, Cash, Love, and Play.
- A four-year high-school chapter (ages 14–17) with Grades, Belonging, Future readiness, yearly commitments, persistent school contacts, and graduation paths.
- Health as a cross-cutting stat and action sheet.
- Three origin presets: Balanced, Academic, and Social.
- Random Life with unlimited seeded previews, plus Custom Life using context/origin templates, one defining trait, and an optional talent without direct stat allocation.
- Shared five-axis personality for the player, partner, friend, teacher, and boss; gradual choice-driven development; compact bars/flags in creation, Identity, NPC inspection, and life review.
- Seeded world/personality/relationship/talent reactions and four capped talent aptitudes that improve ordinary play now while preserving every hard requirement.
- Entry and skilled work tiers, one partner model, recovery windows, basic registry events, deterministic debug-console tooling, local saves, and terminal life review.

## Expanded working-tree scope

- Ordinary career tracks, household debt/retirement, properties and movable assets, partner/family lifecycle, canonical physical and mental health, personality, world conditions, and narrow firearm-linked legal consequences are integrated behind the existing four-tab shell.
- Multi-year continuity threads connect layoffs, debt, caregiving, illness, delinquency, and legal records to the domains that own their downstream effects.

## Deferred

Founder E1 remains the next targeted special-career expansion. Additional special careers, unrestricted social graphs, a standalone crime simulator, cloud saves, analytics, and new top-level simulation domains remain deferred.

New content belongs in the existing action or event registry. New mechanics must preserve the simulator as the only mutation boundary and the dashboard as a read-only surface over simulator state.
